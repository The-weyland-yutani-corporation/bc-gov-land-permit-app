Before you start

Use fictional or non-sensitive information only. This demonstration does not connect to government application systems.

The guidance is preparatory and is not a permit decision or legal advice.

How the service works

Complete a short, reviewable journey. You remain in control of what is saved and can correct your information before viewing guidance.

  1. Describe the project

    Choose a project pathway and provide a general location, activities, timing, and known constraints.

  2. Review information gaps

    See which answers are missing or uncertain before the demonstration evaluates its synthetic rules.

  3. Prepare next steps

    Receive a non-binding readiness summary with assumptions, illustrative sources, and questions to confirm.

What this service provides

The demonstration combines structured intake with plain-language explanations. Deterministic sample rules identify possible requirements; the navigator helps explain them.

A clearer starting point

Organize project details using language designed for applicants, not specialists.

Visible assumptions

Separate what is known, unknown, and illustrative so uncertainty is never hidden by confident wording.

Possible sequencing

See where one authorization may depend on information or activity from another step.

Questions for authorities

Leave with a concise set of items to verify with the responsible permitting bodies.

Important information

Understand the demonstration boundary before entering any project information.

Privacy and identity

The production concept uses a verified sign-in. This design proof uses no real identity provider and should contain no personal, confidential, or commercially sensitive information.

Guidance is non-binding

Results are not an application, approval, legal opinion, or complete statement of requirements. Confirm current obligations directly with the responsible authorities.
